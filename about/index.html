About

Welcome! My name is Thuta Aung, but you can call me Zack. I graduated from the University of Michigan with a BSE in computer engineering. My primary area of focus is firmware and low-level software for low-power embedded systems.

My experience includes vehicle connectivity software engineering at Zoox and embedded sensing projects such as the Smart Sensor Interface and a wearable posture-feedback system. I also cofounded BridgeBurma, a community focused on sharing opportunities and knowledge among Burmese students around the world.

My résumé is currently under construction. You can read my writing or browse the projects on the home page.
