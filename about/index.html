About

I’m Zack Chen, an embedded firmware SDET with about six years owning validation, release, and increasingly the implementation of Telematics Control Unit (TCU) and TUX audio stacks on Zoox’s autonomous vehicle fleet.

I built and still own the TCU validation framework, the firmware flashing and deploy-artifact system, and the release pipeline that carries a vendor drop to the fleet — 49 TCU firmware versions and 22 TUX versions shipped to production. That work has expanded into production C++ middleware, modem and kernel-level root cause analysis, and process design across manufacturing, eSIM activation, and Tier-1 vendor collaboration.

I’m targeting Senior Embedded Middleware & Automation: continuing the shift from test-only ownership into production firmware, system architecture, and cross-organizational process leadership.
