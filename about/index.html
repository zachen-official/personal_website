About

I’m Zack Chen. I earned a B.S.E. in Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan (2019), then built embedded sensing and IoT systems at Evigia and in UMich research before moving into vehicle telematics.

At Zoox I’ve spent about six years on the telematics and in-vehicle audio stacks — writing production C++ middleware, Python build and provisioning systems, and A2B tooling. Audio work has been primarily debugging with Audacity, PulseAudio, ALSA, and the A2B bus. That also includes a cellular voice-readiness feature shipped end to end, the signing and deploy-artifact generator behind 49 telematics and 22 audio firmware releases, and production fault debug down to coredumps, register state, and modem traces.

I entered through validation and moved into implementation, systems architecture, and cross-organizational technical design. I’m targeting Senior Software Development Engineer in either embedded or backend systems.
